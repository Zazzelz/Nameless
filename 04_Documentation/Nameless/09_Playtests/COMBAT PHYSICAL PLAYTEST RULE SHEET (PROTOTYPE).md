## The Wager
At the start of each round, the players will select a wager, the wager dictates which trap cards can be played and what wager bonuses the players receive. Once a round has been completed the Loser will remove their wager HP from their Health Pool (starts with 12HP)
* A high risk wager (⚡⚡⚡ ) 
	* Wagering 5 HP 
	* Allows for some strong card effects
* A medium risk Wager (⚡⚡)
	* Wagering 3 HP
	* Middle ground card effects
* A low risk wager (⚡)
	* Wagering 2 HP
	* Safe choices Safe card effects
## The Deck
There are the cards are split into 3 categories which can be identified as follows
### Phase 1 Cards
* These cards have Purple Details 
* A  ◖ at the top left of the card next to the card name
* These cards are played before the dice are rolled and are designed to influence the dice themselves

### Phase 2 Cards
* These cards have Blue details 
* A ◗ at the top left of the card next to the card name
* These cards are played after the dice have been rolled and are designed to influence the result of the dice
![[image-1.png]]

### Trap Cards
* These Cards have black details 
* A ⬤ at the top left of the card next to the card name
* These cards can be played in either phase 1 or 2 face down 
	* They can only be played when the player has wagered the matching level of risk 
	* They can not be triggered the same round they are placed 
	* You can only play 1 trap card at a time

## **How to Play (Round Structure)**
### **Before starting**
1. build your own deck made up of 15 cards
	1. it is recommended to try have 5 of each (Phase 1, Phase 2, Trap) but feel free to build the deck how you want
2. Each player deals themselves 5 cards to their hand
3. Each Player starts with 12 HP
### **1. Choose a Wager**
Both players secretly choose a wager and reveal. Wagers determine:
- which **wager bonuses** can activate
- which **trap** will trigger next round (if armed)
### **2. Phase 1**
Both players play **one Phase 1 card** face‑up. These modify dice **before** rolling. They can be played at any wager, but only gain their **wager bonus** if the wager matches.
### **3. Roll Dice**
Roll your dice pool, adjusted by Phase 1 effects.
### **4. Phase 2**
Both players play **one Phase 2 card** face‑up. These modify dice **after** rolling. They can be played at any wager, but only gain their **wager bonus** if the wager matches.
### **5. Resolve Totals**
Apply all modifiers. Highest total wins the round. 
* The Loser will remove their wager HP from their total Health Pool
* In the case of a draw no damage is taken on either side
### **Preparing for the Next Round**
- Discard all Phase 1 and Phase 2 cards from the play area into their owner’s discard pile
- Any face-down Traps remain in play until triggered
- Each player draws **1 card**, unless an effect instructs otherwise
    - If a player’s deck is empty, shuffle their discard pile to form a new deck
### **Trigger Traps**
If you armed a trap last round, it becomes **active this round**.  
When its condition is met, **the trap triggers immediately**.
- Traps are played face-down during Phase 1 or Phase 2
- They do nothing the round they are played
- They may trigger **at any point in the next round** when their condition is met
- Only **one trap** may be armed at a time
## **How to Win**
Outplay your opponent and reduce their HP to 0.
